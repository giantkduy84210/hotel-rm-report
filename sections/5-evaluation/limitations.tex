\subsection{Limitations and Assumptions}
\begin{guide}
  \guidepart{Content to include}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Total rooms not available $\to$ estimated from max(rooms\_sold): occupancy may be overstated, and true overbooking is never observed (censored data)
    \item adr = 0 $\to$ complimentary rooms, filtered
    \item Revenue = room revenue only (no F\&B); walk cost is an assumed multiple of ADR
    \item Price elasticity not estimable (no independent price variation in data) $\to$ assumed, shown via tornado
    \item Demand is censored: rejected/unrecorded bookings are unobserved; bookings across nights treated independently; no recapture of turned-away demand
    \item Leakage: \texttt{reservation\_status} known only after the event $\to$ cut-off-based features for overbooking
    \item Season definition (peak Jun--Sep) fits the resort, not the city hotel $\to$ city results used for comparison
    \item 2 hotels in different segments $\to$ run models separately
    \item Canceled bookings: distinguish demand vs revenue
    \item Generalisability: two Portuguese hotels, Jul 2015--Aug 2017
  \end{itemize}
\end{guide}
